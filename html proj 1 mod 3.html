About

Frontend Simplified is an amazing program that teaches you how to code, helps you build your portfolio, assist with interview prepping, and will stick with you until you land your first junior coding job. The best part is that the program is self-paced, modules that are easy to follow, and mentors that are available to assist no matter the time.


This is a great program, but remember: you need to put in the work. Don't rush, take your time on the modules, & be prepared to take a lot of notes. You will definitely want to go the extra mile and hop on the live sessions where the mentors really break things down even more for you. If you can't make it to the live sessions, definitely go back & watch the replays, and again, take plenty of notes. It's a lot of information to soak in, but remember to have fun doing it!
